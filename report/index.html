How scoring & screening works

Stage 1: every ticker must first clear all 7 Graham entry gates (pass/fail) to even be considered:

Valuation

  • Trailing P/E ≤ 20
  • P/E × Price/Book ≤ 30 (Munger's twist on Graham's own margin-of-safety check — paying a fair price for a wonderful business, not just a statistically cheap one)

Financial health

  • Market cap ≥ $2B (avoids fragile small caps)
  • Current ratio ≥ 1.5
  • Debt / equity ≤ 1.0

Profitability

  • 4 consecutive years of positive net income
  • Dividend record: an optional gate, off by default in this system — a quality overlay substitutes for it

Stage 2: stocks clearing Stage 1 must also meet a Munger quality floor before they're eligible for a score:

  • Profitability — return on equity ≥ 15%, gross margin ≥ 30%
  • Cash flow — positive free cash flow

Everything clearing both stages gets a 0–100 Munger score, weighted: return on equity 30%, gross margin 20%, FCF yield 20%, low debt 15%, operating margin 15%. Ranking, not a pass/fail bar — the buy queue takes the top-scoring names within the portfolio's position limits. Full detail: DESIGN.md §3.3.
